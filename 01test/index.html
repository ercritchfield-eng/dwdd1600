Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the ability to navigate, participate in, and use digital technology and online communities safely, responsibly, and ethically.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the legal frameworks, statutes, and ethical regulations that govern how individuals, organizations, and software interact with digital technology and the internet.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a formal set of rules and guidelines established by an organization that defines how its computer network, devices, and digital resources may and may not be used.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's words, ideas, data, creative work, or intellectual property and presenting them as your own without proper attribution or permission.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any intentional or reckless behavior that undermines the fairness, honesty, and truthfulness of the educational process.

What are the rules for AI Generated content?

The rules governing AI-generated content depend on whether you are working in an academic, legal/copyright, or professional/media setting. Across all contexts, the core standard is human accountability and transparency.
